# Class task, Week 1: record an echo

Today you will record one clap in a large space and find its echo. Nothing on this page is marked.

| Today we are learning | |
| --- | --- |
| The idea | An echo is the same sound coming back to you later. |
| By the end you can | Point to an echo in a recording and say how long it took to return. |

## Set it up

1. Open the voice recording app on your computer.
2. Stand about 20 metres from a large flat wall.
3. Press Record.

If it will not open, tell me straight away and use the computer next to you.

## Clap and listen

Clap once, loudly, then stay silent for five seconds.

| Do this | Listen for |
| --- | --- |
| Play the recording back with your headphones on. | One loud clap, then a quieter copy of it. |
| Play it again, and watch the waveform. | The quieter copy lines up with a small second peak. |

> [!tip] Hard to hear?
> Turn your headphones up and clap again, a little further from the wall.
